Full description:
Tazbeeta connects you with one company for your home services in Egypt.

Tell us what you need, add up to 3 photos, and pick the date and time that suit you. The company reviews your request, confirms the time with you, and sends a trained technician.

Why Tazbeeta
- One company, one price: you get a clear quote from the company before any work starts. Prices shown as "starting from" are a guide only; the final quote is confirmed after inspection.
- Choose your own visit date and time.
- Know who is coming: see the assigned technician's name and rating.
- Safe chat: talk to the technician inside the app. Phone numbers stay private.
- Track every step: request received, quote, technician assigned, on the way, work started, completed. You get notifications at each step.
- 30-day warranty on every invoice.
- Pay the company: cash or transfer, confirmed by the company.
- Rate the technician and the service after the job.
- Support on WhatsApp whenever you need it.

Services include finishing and renovation, AC maintenance, electrical appliance repair and more. Tell us what you need even if you do not see it in the list.

For technicians: receive assigned jobs, update the job status, chat with the customer, and see your earnings after each completed job.

Available in Arabic and English.
