Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Tazbeeta app (package com.sallehli.sallehli)

This policy explains what {{COMPANY}} ("we", "the company", operator of the Tazbeeta app) collects when you use the app, why, and the choices you have. Tazbeeta is a home-services app: you request a service, the company inspects and quotes, and a technician assigned by the company does the work.

1. What we collect

  • Account: first and last name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored only as a salted hash, never readable by us).
  • Requests: the service you choose, your description, your address and area, notes, quotes, invoices, payment method and status, ratings and complaints.
  • Location: approximate or precise location, only when you tap "use my location" while the app is open. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos: photos you attach to a request, payment transfer receipts you upload, and before/after photos uploaded by the technician. We only receive the photos you choose; the app does not read your gallery.
  • Messages: chat between you and your technician for a request. The company can view chats to supervise service quality and resolve disputes.
  • Device notification token: a technical identifier so we can send you order status notifications (through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Technicians also have skills, availability and job history.

2. How we use it

To create and manage your account, handle requests, quotes, invoices, payments and warranty, assign a technician, let you and your technician chat, send status notifications and verification emails, support you, prevent abuse, and improve the service. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

3. Who sees it

  • The company (admins) sees account and request data to run the service.
  • Your assigned technician sees your first name, service details, address, photos and chat. Your phone number is not shared with the technician; contact is through the in-app chat. The technician's phone is likewise not shown to you.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, Gmail for verification emails). They process data only to provide those services.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

4. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Access to admin tools is limited to the company. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account inside the app (More, then Delete account) or on the web at /delete-account. On deletion we remove your profile, addresses, location, photos, chat messages and notification tokens, and anonymize your past requests. Invoices and payment records may be kept in anonymized form where needed for accounting and legal reasons.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your details in the app, ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Contact us below. This policy is intended to follow Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020).

7. Children

The app is for people 18 and over and is not directed to children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.

2. كيف نستخدمها

لإنشاء حسابك وإدارته، ومعالجة الطلبات وعروض الأسعار والفواتير والدفع والضمان، وتعيين الفني، والمحادثة بينك وبينه، وإرسال إشعارات الحالة ورسائل التفعيل، ودعمك، ومنع إساءة الاستخدام، وتحسين الخدمة. لا نبيع بياناتك ولا نعرض إعلانات.
